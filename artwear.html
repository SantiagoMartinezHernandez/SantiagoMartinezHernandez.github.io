The Clinical Need

Over 60 million individuals worldwide live with tremor-dominant movement disorders. Pharmacological interventions often suffer from declining efficacy over time, while surgical Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) is highly invasive and carries significant surgical risk.

The ART/Wear Mechatronic Solution

ART/Wear embeds high-density surface electromyography (sEMG) arrays into an ergonomic fabric. Our proprietary algorithm predicts tremor oscillations milliseconds in advance, triggering timed biphasic electrical pulses that desynchronize peripheral afferent pathways and suppress involuntary tremor.

Engineering Deep-Dive

Hardware & Signal Processing Stack

⚡

Afferent Neuromodulation

Proprietary closed-loop current pulses delivered directly to peripheral nerve branches, disrupting phase synchronization in the central tremor network.

🧠

Dense sEMG & Predictive AI

Multi-channel array downsampled with low-latency DSP routines to compute tremor phase and frequency before physical deflection occurs.

🪡

Mechatronic Smart Garment

Shape Deposition Manufacturing (SDM) and conductive flexible polymers embedded into breathable fabric for comfortable daily wear.

📊

Tele-Health Biomarkers

Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) stream syncing quantitative motor biomarkers directly with attending clinicians in Switzerland and worldwide.
